Data and connection
Everything here is saved in this browser (local storage) and survives reloads. Members, tasks, projects, voice history and notifications are fully editable.
To share across a team and send real reminders, connect a backend: sign-in, a Postgres database, a scheduled job for reminders, and a speech/AI API key held server-side. The task and voice logic here is written to move over unchanged.

Owner · Admin · Manager · Member · Viewer. Set per member in Team Members. Server-side enforcement needs the backend.
